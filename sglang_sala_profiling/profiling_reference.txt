  Key Findings

  1. This model is uniquely sensitive to numerical precision. Any kernel that reimplements RMSNorm, changes attention reduction order,
  or uses a different chunk fusion strategy produces different intermediate values that cascade through 32 layers. Only operations that
   are provably bit-exact (like our fused_scaled_add with explicit bf16 rounding) survive.
  2. The 1.5x gap to competitor is NOT in kernel fusion. The profiling shows the system is near-optimal for BF16 serving (97% bandwidth
   utilization on decode). The gap likely comes from: quantizing ALL layers (not just lightning), a custom fla kernel that IS
  numerically identical but faster, or fundamentally different scheduling.
  3. The official package (sglang-minicpm/python) runs Test 1 in 6.81s vs our cp baseline in 50.80s. There may be optimizations in the
  official package we're not using. Worth investigating.
  4. The biggest actionable optimization remaining is quantizing minicpm4 layers to INT4 as well — they account for 58% of W4A16
  prefill time because they're still BF16.
